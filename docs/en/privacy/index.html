Privacy policy

This policy covers Bimmer Dashboard for Android (package bimmer.dashboard) and this website, distinguishing local phone data, voluntary support reports and information processed by Google's advertising services.

1. Data controller and contact

Bimmer Dashboard is provided by Konrad Koszela, using the developer name Kodzix. Konrad Koszela is the controller of information received in support reports and processed in connection with operating the app and this website, within the scope described in this policy.

For privacy, rights requests and support, contact kodzixdev@gmail.com. The app requires no account. Information remaining solely on your device is not automatically disclosed to the controller. Processing by independent providers, including Google, is described below.

2. Vehicle readings and permissions

Bluetooth connects a diagnostic adapter. The app reads engine and transmission parameters, faults and ECU identification, potentially including a VIN supplied by the ECU, adapter MAC address and name, and protocol. Remembered ECU profiles help subsequent connections.

Optional GPS measurements use Android location information: speed, accuracy, time and satellite information. Coordinates and route history are not saved. Older Android versions may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery. A notification and connection service support background readings; they do not automatically upload logs.

3. Information stored locally

The phone stores screen settings and appearance, remembered adapters and ECUs, measurements, histories, a diagnostic log and imported sessions. JSON profiles may include configuration, the adapter and histories; CSV exports contain measurements or a log.

Files are not automatically uploaded to Kodzix. Android may include them in backups or device transfers according to system and provider settings. Three-ad progress and PRO access exist only in process memory and disappear when it ends.

4. Reports, exports and Google AI

Report a bug prepares an email for you to review and send. It includes phone, Android and app-version information, adapter and ECU details, connection status and a CSV copy. The log may include a VIN, MAC address, ECU responses and readings. Sending it also supplies your sender address and added description, used to address the issue.

For ordinary sharing, you choose the recipient. The AI button opens a browser question based on the fault code, description, ECU and language, without attaching a VIN, MAC address or log. Further use of Google and the browser follows their policies.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The advertising-enabled release uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share an IP address, approximate location inferred from that IP, app and advertising interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Purposes include advertising, measurement and analytics, and preventing abuse. The app's code does not pass vehicle readings, VINs, adapter MAC addresses or GPS information to the advertising SDK.

Where required, UMP presents choices before ads are requested. The Ad privacy button lets you reopen the required settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Refusing or withdrawing consent may limit ad availability or personalisation. Three voluntary rewarded ads unlock PRO for the current app-process session.

See how Google uses information from apps and information processed by the SDK.

6. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Providing requested app functions: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where necessary for the service.
  • Support, fault diagnosis, security and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is effective support and protecting rights.
  • Advertising requiring consent: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and applicable device-technology rules. Consent can be withdrawn for the future.

Reports and GPS are optional. Missing permissions limit the functions needing them; missing issue information may prevent diagnosis. Basic dashboard screens remain available without PRO. Google's policies explain its processing grounds.

7. Recipients and international processing

Reports sent to kodzixdev@gmail.com are handled through Google's Gmail service. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. File recipients you select, and Google when you use a browser or Google AI, also apply their own privacy policies. We do not publish received logs or pass them to AdMob.

Recipients may include email providers handling a voluntary report, recipients you choose for exports, Google and advertising partners shown in the consent form, and this website's hosting providers. System backups are handled by the provider selected in Android settings.

Providers may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under their terms and applicable transfer arrangements, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses where applicable. Google's data-transfer frameworks explain its safeguards and how to obtain further information.

8. Retention and deletion

  • The source log covers one session, up to 2 MiB. A new session replaces the previous one; further writing stops when the limit is reached. The last log remains available after disconnection.
  • Attachment copies older than seven days are removed when another export or report is prepared. There is no separate daily deletion schedule.
  • Settings, measurements and imports remain until deleted through an available app function or by clearing app data. You can also use Android settings or uninstall the app. Exports in chosen folders and system backups must be managed separately.
  • Support correspondence and submitted attachments are retained until the report is closed and then for no more than 90 days after closure. A report is considered closed after a final response, or after 30 days without a reply to a request for information needed to proceed. Selected information may be retained longer only when needed for a specific legal claim or legal obligation: until the proceedings are finally concluded, the applicable limitation period expires, or the statutory retention period ends. Email deletion is performed as part of support handling, not by the app.

Google applies its own retention periods and practices. Uninstalling the app does not delete an email already sent or information held by independent providers.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of information, object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact kodzixdev@gmail.com. We have no remote access to information that remains only on your phone.

You may complain to the competent supervisory authority, including Poland's President of the Personal Data Protection Office. We do not use diagnostic logs to make automated decisions producing legal effects. Advertising personalisation by providers depends on their policies and your choices.

We respond to data-rights requests without undue delay, normally within one month of receipt. The period may be extended in the circumstances allowed by the GDPR; we will inform you and explain why. Withdrawing advertising consent does not automatically delete support correspondence processed on a separate legal basis.

10. This website

This website contains no ads, external fonts, or our own analytics tools and advertising tracking identifiers. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes whether or not they are signed into GitHub; it may also process technical browser and request information needed to deliver and protect the service. We do not specify a separate retention period for the provider's systems here. See the GitHub privacy statement and GitHub Pages visitor data information.

11. Security and updates

Local information benefits from Android's protections. A shared attachment receives limited read access; we do not publish logs automatically. Google's SDK connections are encrypted in transit. The security and copies of email or exported files also depend on the provider and recipient you select.

We do not operate our own diagnostic synchronisation server or automatically upload crash reports to Kodzix. This policy may change as features and providers change. The date above identifies the current version.
