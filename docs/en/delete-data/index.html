Request data deletion

How to request deletion of information sent to the developer of Bimmer Dashboard. The app is provided by Konrad Koszela under the developer name Kodzix.

Send an email request

You can request deletion without installing the app or creating an account. Bimmer Dashboard does not offer user accounts.

  1. Email kodzixdev@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Bimmer Dashboard — data deletion.
  3. Ask us to delete information from your support requests, including attached logs. Preferably send the request from the address used for the original report. Include its date or subject if you remember it.

Do not resend the log or send an identity document. If we cannot locate your report or confirm that it concerns you, we will ask for necessary clarification. We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. Where permitted by law, this period may be extended; we will notify you and explain why.

What we delete

After reviewing your request, we delete the covered support information under our control:

  • received support messages and attachments;
  • diagnostic logs and their vehicle, measurement, adapter and device information, including VIN or adapter identifiers if they were submitted;
  • email addresses and other contact information in the deleted correspondence.

Exceptions and retention periods are explained below. We do not have remote access to information stored only on your device.

What may be retained and for how long

Without an earlier deletion request, support correspondence and attachments are kept until the report is closed and for no more than 90 days after closure. A report is closed after a final response or after 30 days without a reply to a request for information needed to proceed. This is a mailbox handling schedule, not automatic deletion by the app.

For a valid request, we delete the information earlier without undue delay, unless selected information is needed for a specific legal claim or legal obligation. Such information may be retained until proceedings are finally concluded, the applicable limitation period expires or the statutory retention period ends. Our response will explain the scope and reason for any retention.

Information on your device and with other providers

You can delete local settings, logs, measurements and imported sessions using the app's relevant controls or Android settings: Apps → Bimmer Dashboard → Storage → Clear data. This also removes your app configuration. Delete exported files in your chosen folders and system backups separately.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not delete messages already sent to us: email the address above for that. Information processed independently by Google or other providers, such as advertising, browser or system-backup data, is subject to their own procedures. For details, see retention periods and the Bimmer Dashboard privacy policy.
